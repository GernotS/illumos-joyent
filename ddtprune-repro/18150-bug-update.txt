18150 ZFS fast deduplication

Port of OpenZFS "Fast Dedup" (feature@fast_dedup,
com.klarasystems:fast_dedup) and the upstream fixes to it, as in
https://code.illumos.org/c/illumos-gate/+/4810 (patchset 40).

New on-disk feature, zpool(8) subcommands and properties:
- feature@fast_dedup: flat-phys DDT entries, a dedup log in front of
  the DDT ZAPs, and per-table directories.  Legacy DDTs keep working and
  can coexist with FDT tables; new tables are FDT once the feature is
  enabled.
- zpool ddtprune -d <days> | -p <percent> <pool>
- zpool prefetch -t ddt <pool>
- pool properties dedup_table_quota and dedup_table_size
- zdb: DDT log, quota and prune accounting

illumos-specific changes (not upstream)
=======================================
- libzfs: zpool ddtprune reports EALREADY ("a prune operation is already
  in progress") instead of zpool_standard_error() turning it into
  EZFS_UNKNOWN and abort().
- ZTS: dedup_ddt_stray_class (hardening against openzfs/zfs#17528, which
  is still open upstream), dedup_prune_all, dedup_prune_deadlock,
  dedup_prune_race, dedup_prune_args, dedup_prune_concurrent,
  dedup_prune_log_flush (DTrace in place of upstream's libzpool test
  program for #19223), dedup_prune_percentage.


Upstream changes not included
=============================
- #14654  larger (32K) DDT ZAP blocks: tuning only; this port keeps 4K.
- #17875  uncached DDT log/BRT I/O: illumos has no DMU_UNCACHEDIO.
- #18055  compressed entry buffer size: fixes upstream's ABD-based
          ddt_zap compression, which this port does not use.
- #18888  ddt_zap_walk() cursor _by_dnode: saves a dnode hold per entry;
          no functional change.
- #19029  kmem_free size of ddt_prune_entry_t: allocation and free sizes
          already match in this port.
- #17926, #18245  zpool status -D -p and dedupused/dedupsaved
          properties: separate features, not part of fast dedup.


Testing
=======
- SmartOS built with 4810 PS40: zfstest -T dedup 21/21 PASS.
- dedup_prune_args, dedup_prune_concurrent and dedup_prune_log_flush
  repeated 6x: 18/18 PASS.
- Before the prune fixes, a standalone reproducer showed -p 1 pruning
  every entry, -d overflow pruning recent entries, two prunes claiming
  the mark concurrently, zpool aborting on EALREADY, and refcnt-2
  entries pruned when the dedup log flushed during a prune (zdb -bcc
  then aborted).  All are fixed in PS40.
